LINKEDIN ABOUT 717 / 2000

Dev Weekends is a free, nonprofit community of engineers, started in 2018 with sessions on weekends, because that is when working people could show up. The name stayed; today something runs almost every day. More than 30,000 people are part of it, from 30 countries. We run a structured Fellowship (2,100+ fellows trained across 2025 and 2026), mentorship, open source through DWOSI and Google Summer of Code prep. In 2026, nine members were selected for Google Summer of Code, and members have earned more than $2.5M through the jobs, contracts and stipends that followed. It is free on purpose: the people who need it most are the ones who cannot pay. Great engineers. Greater impact. Join us at devweekends.com.

Dev Weekends is a free, nonprofit, international community of engineers, founded in 2018. 30,000+ members in 30 countries learn through a structured Fellowship (2,100+ trained), mentorship and open source. We partner with companies, universities and communities to run programs together.
